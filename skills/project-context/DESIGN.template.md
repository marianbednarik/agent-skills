# Design

## Character and experience

_The intended character of the application and the experience it should create for its users. Use concrete references and explain what makes them appropriate, rather than relying on generic adjectives._

## Visual principles

_Agreed principles for hierarchy, density, typography, color, and composition where they guide future design choices. Explain what they optimize for; use examples or counterexamples when they clarify a consequential choice._

## Interaction principles

_Intent behind navigation, motion, feedback, and interaction states. Capture expectations that should remain coherent as the application grows._

## References and existing design system

_Follow the project's existing design-document convention where one is established. Point to authoritative visual references, components, and design tokens without copying their inventories or exact values._
