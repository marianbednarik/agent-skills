# Design

## Character and experience

_The intended character of the application and the experience it should create
for its users._

## Visual principles

_Agreed principles for hierarchy, density, typography, color, and composition
where they guide future design choices. Explain what they optimize for._

## Interaction principles

_Intent behind navigation, motion, feedback, and interaction states. Capture
expectations that should remain coherent as the application grows._

## References and existing design system

_Useful visual references and the reasons they fit. Point to existing components
and design tokens as their sources of truth, without copying their inventories
or exact values._
